# Returns Policy (POL-RET)

## Standard return window
Customers may return most items within **30 days of delivery**. The window starts on the delivery date recorded on the order, not the order date.

## Defective, damaged, wrong, or missing items
Items that arrive damaged, defective, incorrect, or not as described may be returned within **90 days of delivery**. Return shipping is free in these cases.

## Final-sale items
Final-sale items are **not returnable** for change of mind. They remain returnable and replaceable if damaged, defective, wrong, or not as described.

## Non-returnable items
Hygiene products, gift cards, and digital downloads are non-returnable unless faulty.

## Return shipping
Approved returns receive a prepaid label valid for 14 days. Refunds are issued only after the parcel is scanned by the carrier, or immediately for damaged items where the customer supplies a photo.

## Exchanges
Exchanges are handled as a return plus a new order. Replacements are available for defective items within the 90-day defective window.
