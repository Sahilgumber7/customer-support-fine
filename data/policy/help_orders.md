# Help Centre — Orders and Delivery (HELP-ORD)

## Where is my order?
Open your order in the account area to see the live carrier status and the estimated delivery date. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days.

## My tracking has not updated
Tracking can pause for up to 48 hours while a parcel moves between depots. If there has been no scan for 10 business days, contact support for a trace.

## Can I change my address?
Address changes are possible before dispatch. Once an order shows as shipped, the address cannot be changed by support.

## My order arrived incomplete
Check the packing slip first. If an item is genuinely missing, report it within 30 days and we will send a replacement.

## How do I cancel?
You can cancel while the order still shows as placed. After dispatch, use the returns process.
