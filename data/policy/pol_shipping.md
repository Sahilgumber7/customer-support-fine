# Shipping Policy (POL-SHIP)

## Delivery estimates
Standard delivery is 3–5 business days. Express is 1–2 business days. Estimates are not guarantees.

## Delays
If a parcel is more than **3 business days past its estimated delivery date**, the agent may open a carrier trace and offer a replacement or refund at the customer's choice.

## Lost parcels
A parcel is considered lost after **10 business days** without a tracking scan. The agent may then issue a replacement at no cost.

## Address changes
Addresses can be changed only while an order is in the placed state. Shipped orders cannot be redirected by support.

## Tracking
Tracking numbers are provided on the order record. Support must read the carrier status from the system rather than guessing.
