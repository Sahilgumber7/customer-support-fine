# FAQ — Products and Payments (FAQ-GEN)

## How do I know which size to order?
Product pages list measurements in centimetres. When between sizes, choose the larger size for outerwear.

## Are your products compatible with my device?
Compatibility is listed under the product specifications. If a specification is missing, support can confirm from the product record.

## What payment methods do you accept?
Major cards and store credit. Cash on delivery is available for orders under $200.

## I was charged twice
Duplicate authorisations usually reverse within 3 business days. If both charges settle, support can raise a payment investigation and refund the duplicate.

## How long do refunds take?
Refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear within 5–7 business days.

## Do you price match?
We do not price match, but items reduced within 14 days of purchase can be adjusted once.
