# Refunds Policy (POL-REF)

## Eligibility
A refund requires a delivered order, a reason within the return policy, and no prior refund covering the same value.

## Limits
Refunds above **$150.00** require human approval before they are issued. Refunds above **$500.00** are escalated for specialist review.

## Duplicate refunds
An order cannot be refunded twice. If a refund is pending or processed for the full order value, further refund requests are declined.

## Timing
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**. The support agent must never promise an instant refund.

## Partial refunds
Where an order contains multiple items, the customer must identify which item the refund relates to. Refund amounts are calculated from the recorded item price, never from a customer-supplied figure.

## Cancellations
An order can be cancelled while it is still in the **placed** state. Once shipped, it must go through the returns process instead.
