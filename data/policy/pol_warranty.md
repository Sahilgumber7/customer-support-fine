# Warranty Policy (POL-WAR)

## Coverage
Product warranties start on the delivery date. The warranty period for each item is listed on its product record.

## Claims
A warranty claim requires a delivered order, the item SKU, and a description of the fault. Claims inside the warranty period are approved for repair or replacement.

## Exclusions
Warranty does not cover accidental damage, unauthorised repair, or normal wear. Water damage is excluded unless the product is advertised as waterproof.

## After expiry
Expired warranties cannot be claimed. The agent may offer a paid repair referral but must not promise a free replacement.
